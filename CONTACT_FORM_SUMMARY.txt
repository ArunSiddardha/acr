================================================================================
                            QUICK START
================================================================================

1. Configure Form Submission:
   - Sign up at https://formspree.io
   - Get your form ID
   - Update contact.html line 28 with your form ID

2. Test Locally:
   - Open contact-test.html in a browser
   - Or run: python3 -m http.server 8000
   - Visit: http://localhost:8000/contact-test.html

3. Deploy:
   - Build Jekyll site: bundle exec jekyll build
   - Deploy _site folder to your hosting
   - Access via: yoursite.com/contact.html
