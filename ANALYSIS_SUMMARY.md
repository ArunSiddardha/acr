### NVIDIA Stock Analysis

**Historical Context:**
The data captures NVIDIA's incredible journey from a $0.04 stock in 1999 to $125+ in 2024, representing one of the most successful tech investments in history.

**Key Periods:**
- **1999-2000:** Dot-com boom (+138% in 1999, +40% in 2000)
- **2001:** Massive growth (+347%) as NVIDIA established market dominance
- **2002:** Dot-com crash (-83% decline)
- **2008:** Financial crisis (-76% decline)
- **2016-2017:** AI/Deep Learning boom (+233% in 2016, +90% in 2017)
- **2020-2021:** Pandemic tech surge (+118% in 2020, +124% in 2021)
- **2023-2024:** AI Revolution (+246% in 2023, +160% in 2024 YTD)

**Investment Perspective:**
- $1,000 invested in 1999 would be worth ~$3.3 million in 2024
- Demonstrates power of long-term holding through volatility
- Multiple boom-bust cycles navigated successfully
