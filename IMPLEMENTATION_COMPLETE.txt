🚀 QUICK START
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  1. Sign up at https://formspree.io (free)
  2. Create a new form and get your form ID
  3. Edit contact.html line 28:
     Replace: action="https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_FORM_ID"
     With:    action="https://formspree.io/f/[your-actual-id]"
  
  4. Test locally:
     python3 -m http.server 8000
     Open: http://localhost:8000/contact-test.html
  
  5. Deploy your Jekyll site and you're done! 🎉
